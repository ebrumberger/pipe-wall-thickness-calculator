Step 2 Pipe Material & Yield Strength (S)

49 CFR § 192.107 ↗
Engineering Guidance: Grade Selection Tradeoffs API Spec 5L ↗

• Grade B & X42 (S ≤ 42,000 psi): Ideal for pump/compressor stations, meter skids, and manifolds. Heavier walls ease field fitting and resist vibration.

• X52 & X60 (S = 52,000 – 60,000 psi): Industry standard balance for mainline cross-country pipelines and road crossings; excellent field weldability.

• X70 & X80 (S ≥ 70,000 psi): Thinner wall saves freight/tonnage on long runs, but requires qualified automated welding procedures, preheat controls, and Charpy V-notch fracture toughness screening.

Step 3 Regulatory Derating Factors (F, E, T)

49 CFR §§ 192.111–115 ↗
How to choose E: Joint Factor Table ↗
Modern line pipe (API 5L HFERW, DSAW, or Seamless) qualifies for E = 1.00. Pre-1970 legacy pipe (flash welded or furnace butt welded) requires derating to 0.80 or 0.60.
How to choose T: Temperature Derating Table ↗
Buried pipelines operate near ambient soil temperatures (40°F – 90°F), so T = 1.000 is standard. Derating only applies downstream of high-ratio compressors or heated heavy crude transport systems.
